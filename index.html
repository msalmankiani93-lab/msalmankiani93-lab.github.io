Research focus

Two questions, one thread: how do we make clean energy materials work better?

My work runs from the lab bench to the supercomputer — fabricating next-generation thin-film solar cells, and predicting the properties of energy materials before they are ever synthesised.

01 · Experimental

Flexible & printed perovskite solar cells

Solution-processed SnO₂ quantum-dot electron transport layers for lightweight, roll-to-roll compatible photovoltaics — advancing portable and bifacial optoelectronic devices, and untangling the hysteresis that limits their performance.

SnO₂ quantum dots Slot-die coating ETL engineering Photodetectors Device physics
02 · Computational

First-principles design of energy materials

Density functional theory studies of perovskite-type hydrides (XYH₃) for solid-state hydrogen storage, plus the elastic, electronic, optical and thermoelectric behaviour of oxides under pressure — screening candidates for a hydrogen economy.

DFT Hydrogen storage Hydride perovskites Thermoelectrics Band structure
