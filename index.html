space-sm: 0.5rem space-md: 1rem space-lg: 1.5rem space-xl: 2.5rem space-2xl: 4rem space-3xl: 6rem --- ## Brand & Style This design system establishes an authoritative, scholarly, and modern scientific presence for high-level academic research. Balancing the rigor of materials science and optoelectronics with editorial grace, the visual language draws inspiration from premier peer-reviewed journals, archival publications, and cutting-edge laboratory repositories. The aesthetic philosophy centers on: - **Intellectual Gravitas:** Anchored by profound navy and slate tones, evoking institutions of higher learning, formal defense, and archival credibility. - **Solar Luminescence:** Subtle, precise touches of amber and solar gold serve as energetic highlights, directly referencing perovskite solar technology, photonics, and optoelectronic energy harvesting. - **Editorial Precision:** Broad academic white space, high-contrast serif headlines, and strictly structured geometric sans-serif data points guarantee that complex scientific achievements, citations, and metrics remain immediately legible and dignified. ## Colors The palette is engineered to present deep scholastic authority while maintaining high visual contrast on archival paper backgrounds: - **Primary (`#1C2541` - Deep Slate Navy):** The structural backbone. Applied across primary actions, dominant section headers, structural rules, and primary iconography. - **Secondary (`#D97706` - Optoelectronic Amber):** Represents solar energy, optoelectronic excitement, and high-impact scientific discoveries. Reserved for active navigation indicators, key metrics, award highlights, and DOI links. - **Tertiary (`#0B132B` - Archival Deep Obsidian):** Used for maximum typographic contrast on lead headings, high-level impact counters, and formal publication titles. - **Neutral (`#64748B` - Slate Metadata Grey):** Applied to technical subtitles, bibtex metadata, journal impact factors, publication dates, and standard borders. - **Canvas & Surface Foundations:** - Base canvas: `#F8FAFC` (Crisp Technical Paper) - Card/Container surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure Optical White) - Muted container fill: `#F1F5F9` (Scholarly Field Slate) ## Typography The typographical hierarchy creates a clear dichotomy between scholarly discourse and empirical data: - **The Editorial Serif (`Newsreader`):** Selected for its intellectual lineage, literary clarity, and formal grace. Newsreader serves display headlines, research narrative introductions, paper titles, and key quotes. It preserves humanistic warmth and traditional academic authority. - **The Systematic Sans-serif (`Inter`):** Selected for neutral, high-density legibility across small data points, abstracts, tabular data, authorship lists, and user interface controls. - **Stylistic Conventions:** - Research paper citations italicize the journal name and volume using `Newsreader`. - Author listings, metrics, affiliations, and DOIs strictly utilize `Inter` with slightly enhanced tracking on uppercase metadata labels (`label-sm`). ## Layout & Spacing This design system utilizes an asymmetric, content-first 12-column grid system bounded by generous scholarly margins: - **Desktop Layout (≥ 1024px):** Max container width of 1280px with 64px (`margin-desktop`) canvas padding and 32px (`gutter-desktop`) inter-column separation. Research overview panels use an asymmetric 4/8 column split (4 columns for profile sticky metrics/indices, 8 columns for narrative and publication feeds). - **Tablet Layout (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 32px margin and 24px gutter. Metric rows collapse to a 2x2 grid. - **Mobile Layout (< 768px):** Single-column vertical flow with 20px (`margin`) edge protection and 16px vertical element spacing. - **Academic Rhythm:** Generous vertical rhythm (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) separates thematic domains (e.g., from Biography to Ongoing Research Initiatives to Peer-Reviewed Works) to simulate reading an unhurried, distinguished broadsheet or journal supplement. ## Elevation & Depth To preserve an authentic scientific and editorial feel, this design system avoids heavy shadows, blurred skeumorphism, or high-gloss effects. Instead, depth is constructed through **architectural paper layers and low-contrast borders**: - **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#F8FAFC` base academic canvas. - **Level 1 (Paper Cards & Modules):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a subtle hairline rule: `1px solid #E2E8F0`. No shadow in rest state. - **Level 2 (Hover State & Interactive Cards):** Subtle upward micro-lift accompanied by an ambient, highly-diffused slate shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(28, 37, 65, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 37, 65, 0.04)`. The hairline border subtly shifts from `#E2E8F0` to `#CBD5E1`. - **Level 3 (Modals, BibTeX Drawers, Nav Overlays):** Elevation `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.12)`, anchored with a crisp `1px solid #CBD5E1` boundary. ## Shapes The shape system adopts a refined, academic restraint (`roundedness: 1`): - **Default Element Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to buttons, search inputs, badges, publication tags, and research metric containers. Gives an architectural, crisp paper edge without feeling blunt or aggressive. - **Card & Structural Surface Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to publication cards, featured research module wrappers, and interactive topic panels. - **Pill Exception (`rounded-full`):** Strictly reserved for micro status tags (e.g., "Open Access", "Peer-Reviewed", "Under Review") to differentiate categorical markers from functional interactive cards. ## Components ### Buttons - **Primary:** Filled `#1C2541` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.25rem` radius, `0.75rem 1.5rem` padding. Fonts set in `label-md`. Hover triggers `#0B132B` with a sharp transition. - **Secondary / Solar Accent:** Bordered in `#D97706` with `#D97706` text. Hover transitions to `#D97706` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Used for highlighted calls-to-action (e.g., "Download Full CV", "Access Perovskite Lab Data"). - **Ghost / Editorial:** Transparent background, `#1C2541` text with an underline appearing on hover. Used for citations and paper preprint links. ### Publication Cards - **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E8F0` border, `1.5rem` padding, `0.5rem` border radius. - **Header:** Year and journal title formatted in `label-sm` (`#64748B`), paired with an amber "Featured" or "Open Access" tag. - **Title:** `headline-sm` using `Newsreader`, `#0B132B`. Hover triggers secondary solar amber `#D97706`. - **Authors:** `body-sm` using `Inter`, highlighting "M. S. Kiani" in bold primary navy `#1C2541`. - **Actions Row:** Embedded DOI pill button, citation count badge, and expandable "BibTeX / Abstract" toggle button. ### Metric Counters (h-index, Citations, Grants) - **Container:** Subtle off-white box (`#F8FAFC`), bordered with `#E2E8F0`. - **Number:** Large display numeral (`headline-lg`) in `#1C2541`. - **Label:** `label-sm` uppercase text in `#64748B`, tracking `0.04em`. - **Accent Indicator:** A top hairline border of `2px solid #D97706` to indicate verified live scientific impact. ### Interactive Topic Tabs - **Tabs:** Horizontal bar with an archival underline indicator. Active tab features `#1C2541` text with a `2px solid #D97706` bottom border offset by `0.5rem`. Inactive tabs render in `#64748B`. ### Badges & Tags - **DOI & Index Badges:** Compact height (`1.5rem`), `label-sm` font size, background `#F1F5F9`, text `#1C2541`, with a `1px solid #CBD5E1` border. - **Impact Badge:** Background `#FEF3C7` (soft solar amber wash), text `#92400E`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`. ### Form Fields & Contact Callouts - **Inputs:** Crisp white canvas, `1px solid #CBD5E1` border, `0.25rem` radius. Focus shifts border to `#1C2541` with a `2px` subtle ambient ring in `#1C2541` at 10% opacity. - **Contact Panel:** Framed callout box using `#0B132B` deep background with `#FFFFFF` Newsreader typography and `#D97706` email/institutional links, providing an authoritative closure to the portfolio.
